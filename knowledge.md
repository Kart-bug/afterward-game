\# Project: Afterward (working title)



A first-person psychological horror game for Windows PC, adapting Edith Wharton's 1910 story "Afterward". Released on itch.io. Team of three complete beginners.



\## Fixed decisions

\- Engine: Godot 4.7.x (Standard, GDScript only, no C#). All three teammates use the same version.

\- Renderer: Forward+ (required for built-in volumetric fog). Use fog thoughtfully and test performance on all three PCs. Do not switch renderers without asking the team.

\- Style: first-person, stylized low-fi psychological horror, with VHS/analog textures and shaders inspired by the general visual mood of Fears to Fathom. Use original assets; do not copy its assets or scenes.

\- Budget: zero. Use only free assets with licences that allow commercial-use and itch.io release. Record the licence of every asset in docs/asset-licences.md.

\- Target machine: modest PC (Ryzen 7 CPU, integrated graphics). Keep things light.



\## Not decided yet (do NOT invent or add)

\- Gameplay mechanics, enemies, puzzles, inventory, jump scares, ending design, extra story content.

\- These are chosen by the team later. If a task needs one, ask first.
