\# Stay faithful to the source



The source is Edith Wharton's "Afterward" (1910). The team's reference notes are in docs/ (the team will add them).



\- Never invent plot events, characters, quotes or endings and present them as being from the story.

\- Never write text and claim it is Wharton's. Use only text the team has pasted into docs/ from the verified public-domain edition.

\- Keep three categories clearly separate in everything you write: (1) FACT from the story, (2) INTERPRETATION by the team, (3) ADAPTATION CHOICE for the game.

\- If asked for new dialogue, label it "new game text", never "from the story".

\- If you are not sure whether something is in the story, say you are not sure and ask the team to check the text.

\- Do not use text from modern editions, translations, films or other games.
